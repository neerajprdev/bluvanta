FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. Project discussions and updates can be handled online by WhatsApp, email or an agreed meeting method.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on pages, design, features and integrations. A written quotation is provided after understanding the requirements.

Are domain and hosting included?

They are not automatically included. Domain, hosting, paid plugins, APIs and other third-party costs should be listed clearly in the quotation.

How do I start?

Send a message through WhatsApp or the contact page with a short description of your project.
